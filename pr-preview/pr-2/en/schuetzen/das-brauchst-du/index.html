One year later: Mia and Selin are 12 and now in class 7b. In this chapter you protect Mia's files and devices. For this you need two things that you already know. For each one, ask yourself: can I do this already?

Computing systems in everyday life

I can recognise computing systems in my everyday life, for example a smartphone, a games console or a till. Mia's tablet, her gaming account on the internet and her cloud storage are systems like that. Each of them can be protected, and each can be attacked.

I can tell hardware and software apart and explain how they work together. You will need this soon: malware is software too, and updates change the software, not the hardware.
